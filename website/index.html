What's in the drawer

Hold files

File shelf
Drag files in to park them, with real thumbnails. Drag them out again into Finder, Mail or Slack.
Instant AirDrop
Drop files on the AirDrop tab and the share sheet opens with them already attached.
Opens when you drag
Pull a file toward the top of the screen and the drawer opens to catch it.

Write things down

Quick notes
A full-width notepad that saves as you type.
Save to Apple Notes
One click copies the note into Notes, so it syncs to your iPhone over iCloud.

Stay focused

Pomodoro timer
Focus and break sessions with a chime at the end. The countdown stays visible when the drawer is closed.
Hides in full screen
Movies, games and presentations get the whole screen. Notchify steps aside.

Glance and go

Music controls
See what Apple Music or Spotify is playing and pause it without switching apps.
Camera mirror
A quick look before a call, flipped like a real mirror. Off until you turn it on.
Two buttons you choose
Pick any two of Capture, Lock and Pomodoro. Capture hides the drawer before it takes the shot.

Install in about a minute

  1. Download the disk image

    Get Notchify.dmg and double-click it to open.

  2. Drag it to Applications

    Drop Notchify into your Applications folder. There's no installer.

  3. Open it and hover the notch

    Launch Notchify from Spotlight. Move your pointer to the notch and the drawer opens.

If macOS says it can't verify the developer

Notchify isn't sold through the App Store, so macOS asks first. Right-click the app and choose Open, or run this in Terminal:

xattr -cr /Applications/Notchify.app

Questions

What is Notchify?

A small macOS app that turns the MacBook notch into a drawer. Hover the notch and you get a file shelf, AirDrop, quick notes, music controls, a camera mirror and a Pomodoro timer.

Is it free?

Yes. Notchify is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Does Notchify collect any data?

No. There are no analytics or trackers. Your notes, shelved files and camera image stay on your Mac.

How does saving to Apple Notes work?

In the Notes tab, click the save button and Notchify creates a new note in Apple Notes. The first time, macOS asks whether Notchify may control Notes; choose OK. If it can't reach Notes, the note is copied to your clipboard instead so nothing is lost.

Does the timer keep running when I close the drawer?

Yes. Start a focus session, close the drawer and keep working. The countdown stays next to the notch until the session ends.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 15.6 or later. On MacBooks with a notch it lives in the notch; on other displays it shows a small pill at the top of the screen instead.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the developer?

Notchify is distributed outside the App Store. Right-click the app and choose Open, or run xattr -cr /Applications/Notchify.app in Terminal.
